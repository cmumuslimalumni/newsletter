Assalamu Alaikum
 

Alhamdulillah, it’s been a full few months. As the year wraps up, we wanted to share what the network has been up to since our first issue.

Since then, MAN@CMU has gathered alumni in the DMV, New York, and the Bay Area, welcomed an alum back to campus to sit down with current students, and partnered with MSA on a resume workshop and alumni panel. Seeing Muslim Tartans across cities and graduating classes meet one another has been a real reminder of why we started this.

Before the recaps, though, our most important ask of the year: Giving CMU Day is coming up on Tuesday, December 1. It’s a single 24-hour window when the whole CMU community gives to the parts of campus it cares about, and it’s the best chance we have to show up, together, for Muslim life at CMU. Every gift counts toward participation, so whether you give a little or a lot, please give.

Below you’ll find how to give and where your gift goes, followed by event recaps and highlights of a current student and a fellow alum. Thank you for being part of a great first year, and we’re looking forward to what’s next, inshallah.

— The MAN @ CMU Board

Back on Campus
 
Lunch with Talha Faiz
MechE ’23 · Co-Founder, Candid Intelligence

CMU alum Talha Faiz (MechE ’23), Co-Founder of Candid Intelligence, came back to campus for a small-group lunch with students and fellow alumni. Talha shared his journey at CMU, his experience building a startup, and his work in the energy sector.

The conversation covered entrepreneurship, career pathways after CMU, and the experiences that have shaped Talha’s professional journey — with plenty of time for students’ questions and for alumni and students to connect.

 
“I really enjoyed meeting Talha and getting the opportunity to get insider information on how tech companies and startups work… I enjoyed the casual setting of being at a restaurant and being with other CMU students.”
— Remonda Hassan, Chemical Engineering ’YY
 
Resume Workshop & Alumni Panel
With CMU MSA · September 11

We teamed up with CMU MSA for a resume workshop, starting with a discussion of what makes a resume stand out.

After that, a panel of alumni took open questions from students about their experiences and life after college.

Panelists:

•Samiha Islam, MS Public Policy & Data Analytics, Dec. ’25
•Haqq Coulibaly, BS Materials Science & Engineering ’25 · Engineer @ Asbury Advanced Materials
•Rimsha Ahmed, BS Civil & Environmental Engineering and Engineering & Public Policy ’22 · Energy Engineer @ Energetics
•Sumayya Syeda, BS Electrical & Computer Engineering, Robotics ’24 · Software Engineer @ Apple

Student Highlight
 
Headshot
Ali Tayeb
Computer Science ’29 · ScottyLabs · MSA · Cubing Club

Ali Tayeb is a Computer Science sophomore at Carnegie Mellon working on ML systems — making models run faster and cheaper. Ali is currently doing research on reasoning and video generation, and on campus is involved with ScottyLabs, the MSA, and the Cubing Club.

This summer, Ali was in San Francisco working on inference at Overshoot (YC W26), mostly getting GPU servers up and serving faster. Meeting a lot of investors and builders out there also sparked an interest in venture capital.
